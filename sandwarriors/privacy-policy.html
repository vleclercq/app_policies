Sand Warriors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sand Warriors is an offline arcade game. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network access: the game never connects to the internet.
  • Your progress (best score per mission) is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your campaign progress and best scores are saved locally in the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't run ads or ad networks.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any identifier.
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties.

Children's privacy

Sand Warriors does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app has no accounts and stores no personal data, no such data is collected regardless of the player's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Sand Warriors — Politique de confidentialité

Dernière mise à jour : 5 octobre 2026

Sand Warriors est un jeu d'arcade hors ligne. Il ne collecte, ne stocke, ne transmet ni ne partage aucune donnée personnelle.

  • Aucun compte ni inscription.
