Sand Warriors — Support

Sand Warriors is a retro run-and-gun arcade game: five missions across the desert, an enemy base, a jungle camp, Thunder Road and the jungle sky.

Found a bug, stuck on a mission, or have a suggestion? Write to vleclercq@gmail.com.
Please mention your device model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Move: left virtual stick.
  • Aim: right virtual stick. Fire is automatic.
  • Buttons: throw a grenade, combat roll, and the nuke (clears the screen).
  • Missions unlock one after another on the campaign map; your best score is kept for each.
  • Gamepads and keyboards are supported too.

FAQ

Does the game need an internet connection? No, it plays fully offline.

Is there any purchase or advertising? No.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

Sand Warriors collects no data. See the privacy policy.
