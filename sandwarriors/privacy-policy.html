Sand Warriors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sand Warriors is an offline arcade game. Mission 1 is free; the full game is a one-time in-app purchase. This policy explains the very little data involved, and why.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The only network activity is the in-app purchase: buying or restoring the full game through the App Store, verified by our purchase provider, RevenueCat.
  • Your progress (best score per mission) is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your campaign progress and best scores are saved locally in the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. The transaction is handled by the Apple App Store (or Google Play) and verified through our purchase infrastructure provider, RevenueCat, which receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated for your install (not your name, email, or Apple/Google account).
  • Your purchase status and the transaction details needed to unlock the game (product ID, purchase date).
  • Basic device/platform information (OS, app version) needed to process the purchase.

We never receive your payment details: those stay with Apple or Google. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't run ads or ad networks.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any identifier.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties beyond the purchase processing described above.

Children's privacy

Sand Warriors does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts and stores no personal data, regardless of the player's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Sand Warriors — Politique de confidentialité

Dernière mise à jour : 5 octobre 2026

Sand Warriors est un jeu d'arcade hors ligne. La mission 1 est gratuite ; le jeu complet est un achat intégré unique.

  • Aucun compte ni inscription.
  • Aucune publicité, aucun traçage, aucun SDK d'analyse ou de rapport de plantage.
  • Seul accès réseau : l'achat intégré (achat ou restauration du jeu complet via l'App Store), vérifié par notre prestataire RevenueCat, qui reçoit un identifiant anonyme et l'état de l'achat. Vos données de paiement restent chez Apple ou Google.
  • Votre progression (meilleur score par mission) est enregistrée uniquement sur votre appareil, et supprimée avec l'application.
  • Aucune donnée personnelle n'est collectée, y compris auprès d'enfants.
