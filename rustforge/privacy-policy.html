🦀 Rustforge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Rustforge is an offline Rust-learning game. This policy explains what data the app collects — which is very little — and why.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads, no ad tracking, no analytics SDKs.
  • Your progress (stars, XP, completed levels) is stored only on your device.
  • The only network activity is checking your purchase status with the app store and our purchase provider, RevenueCat.

Data stored on your device

Your campaign progress, stars, and XP are stored locally on your device using standard app storage. This data never leaves your device except as described below, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Purchases

Rustforge Pro is a one-time, non-subscription purchase that unlocks every world beyond World 1. When you purchase or restore Pro, the transaction is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and verified through our purchase infrastructure provider, RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated for your install (not your name, email, or Apple/Google account).
  • Your purchase/entitlement status and transaction metadata needed to unlock Pro (e.g. product ID, purchase date).
  • Basic device/platform information (OS, app version) needed to process the purchase.

We do not receive or store your payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy for how they process this data.

What we don't do

  • We don't run ads or ad networks.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, or photos.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties beyond the purchase processing described above.

Children's privacy

Rustforge does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app has no accounts and stores no personal data, no such data is collected regardless of the user's age.

Data retention & deletion

Local progress data is deleted when you uninstall the app. We hold no server-side personal data to delete. To remove your purchase-history record from RevenueCat, contact us at the address below and we will forward the request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
